# VHALDREN — EPISODE 2: THE SILENT KINGDOM

*What if the knight found a kingdom where nobody could die?*

A standalone ~63-second vertical (9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps) dark-fantasy short. It is the second episode of the original VHALDREN series and stars the same wandering knight, **Cael Vorn**, from Episode 1 (*The Last Ember*). The story is new; it is not a sequel.

---

## H. Character consistency: Cael Vorn

Cael is **not redesigned and not re-modelled.** Episode 2's renderer imports Episode 1's character code (`the-last-ember/render/src/characters.js`, `buildKnight()`), materials, textures and retro shader engine directly. Both episodes therefore draw him from the same vertices, textures and colours. Only his actions, camera angles and environment lighting change.

Reference sheet: [`../images/00_cael_vorn_character_sheet.png`](../images/00_cael_vorn_character_sheet.png). It shows front, three-quarter, side, back, three-quarter back, the helm, the helm profile with its left spur, and the lantern and sigil.

> **[CAEL]** Cael Vorn, a tall lean wandering knight with realistic proportions and a slight weary hunch. He wears blackened charcoal iron plate over a muted-brown quilted gambeson, with lamellar iron scales at the waist and thighs. His **keel-prow helm** is faceted with a forward-pointed prow face, a tall thin keel fin from brow to nape, a sallet-like back flare, a single swept-back spur on the **left** temple only, a glowing ember-orange chevron (V) eye-slit, and a small faded-brass diamond sigil on the brow. Three layered iron plates guard the **right** shoulder; a tattered **oxblood half-cloak** hangs over the **left**. A short dark-crimson tabard bears the **Weeping Ember** sigil (faded-gold diamond, ember dot, three gold ash-tears). A hexagonal iron lantern with a live ember hangs at his left hip. **Vowbrand** is a long single-edged dark-iron blade with a hooked back-spur, a glowing ember vein, an open octagonal brass ring-guard and a leather grip.

### New designs introduced in this episode
- **Aurelmoor, the Silent Kingdom:** a walled valley city beyond the frozen peaks. Cold blue stone, snow-laden roofs, towers with golden window grids, the great central **Wakebell** tower with an open belfry and octagonal spire, a plaza with a **frozen fountain** (tiered basins, icicles, a frozen jet).
- **The Stilled:** tall slender inhabitants in long slate-blue hooded mantles with tarnished-gold hems, a gold placket and a gold clasp. Their faces are smooth, featureless ivory ovals, and snow lies on their hoods and shoulders.
- **The Wakebell:** a colossal bronze bell with engraved brass bands, hung in the great tower.
- **The Stillwork:** an ancient machine in a pillared cylindrical vault beneath the plaza. It has three gimbal rings of glyph-engraved brass around a golden core, an **iris of eight brass petals** that opens like an eye, wall gears, pistons, and hundreds of golden threads binding the core to every soul in the city.

## E. Individual animation instructions

Add to each: *"keep Cael Vorn identical to the reference sheet; retro low-poly animation with segmented rigid limbs and slightly stiff early-2000s motion; no morphing; 30 fps."*

1. **S01:** A 1.5 m push into the blank face over 5.5 s; the lantern flickers; snow drifts; the figure never moves.
2. **S02:** Reverse tracking at walking pace; a heavy, hunched gait against the wind; the cloak flaps hard; snow streaks sideways.
3. **S03:** Cael stands still with the cloak stirring; the camera pushes past him and tilts down to the city; golden lights twinkle.
4. **S04:** Cael walks through the gate toward a low camera that drifts back.
5. **S05:** The camera tracks behind and above Cael down the street; every figure stays perfectly still.
6. **S06:** A slow dolly across the snowy shoulders of a seated figure while Cael passes in the background.
7. **S07:** The figure's head turns over 1.4 s (an ease-in stone creak) until the blank face meets the camera; the camera pushes in.
8. **S08:** Heads, then bodies, turn toward Cael in a wave spreading outward from him, over about 2.5 s.
9. **S09:** He raises Vowbrand into guard in 0.4 s; the V-slit and ember vein flare.
10. **S10:** The bell swings back and strikes; a golden ring expands across the sky over 1.6 s; snow bursts from the roofs; heavy shake.
11. **S11:** Fourteen figures walk inward on staggered timings and stop in a ring; Cael turns side to side in guard.
12. **S12:** They kneel in a staggered wave; light grows beneath the paving; a low camera orbit.
13. **S13:** Eight paving petals hinge open, the fountain sinks 7.5 m, golden light and motes pour upward, and the camera rises.
14. **S14:** The rings turn on three axes, the gears rotate and the pistons pump; the camera pans from Cael on the balcony to the machine.
15. **S15:** Everything stalls for half a second; the iris petals slide open over 1.2 s; then the gears resume faster; a pulse of light.
16. **S16a:** A slow spiral looking up the threads; motes travel upward; the threads shimmer.
17. **S16b:** Cael walks toward the core with the camera tracking behind him.
18. **S17:** He raises the sword overhead over 1.8 s; the shake builds and dust falls; **cut to black at 0:57.4 at the top of the swing**.

### How it was produced

No AI image or video generation model is reachable from this production environment, so, as in Episode 1, every image and every frame was **rendered from a real low-poly 3D production**:
- three.js in headless Chromium with the shared PS1/PS2 shader engine (affine UVs, vertex snapping, Gouraud lighting, fog, Bayer dither, 15-bit colour, bloom) at 360×640;
- nearest-neighbor 3× upscale, low-resolution grain, and burned-in Cinzel subtitles;
- Kokoro narration (Apache-2.0) and a fully procedural score and sound design.

Rebuild with `../build.sh`.

**Rights:** all story, characters, designs, visuals, music, sounds and text are original to this project. Tools: three.js (MIT), Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0), Cinzel (SIL OFL 1.1), ffmpeg.
