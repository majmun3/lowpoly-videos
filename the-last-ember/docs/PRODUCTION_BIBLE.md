# THE LAST EMBER — Production Bible

*What if a wandering knight became the final guardian of a dying world?*

An original dark-fantasy short (≈64 s, vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps) styled as a lost PS1/PS2-era game cinematic.
Everything here is original: the world, characters, creatures, architecture, sigils, music, sound and text.
The reference images were used only for general mood: gothic ruins, a dying sun, knightly armor, crimson skies, retro 3D.
Nothing in the film copies a character, emblem, weapon, location or soundtrack from any existing franchise.

## D. Original character and world designs

### Cael Vorn, the Wanderer of the Grey Road (protagonist)
Canonical descriptor (repeated verbatim in every prompt below as **[CAEL]**):

> **[CAEL]** Cael Vorn, a tall lean wandering knight with realistic human proportions and a slight weary hunch. He wears blackened charcoal iron plate over a muted-brown quilted gambeson, with lamellar iron scales at the waist and thighs. His original **keel-prow helm** is faceted and comes to a forward-pointed prow like a ship's bow. A tall thin keel fin runs from brow to nape, the back flares like a sallet, and a single swept-back iron spur sits on the **right** temple only. Its eye-slit is a glowing ember-orange chevron (V-shape), with a small faded-brass diamond sigil riveted above it. Three layered iron plates guard the **left** shoulder only. A tattered **oxblood half-cloak** hangs over the **right** shoulder. His short dark-crimson tabard bears the **Weeping Ember** sigil: a faded-gold diamond outline, an ember-orange centre dot and three gold "ash-tear" lines below. A small hexagonal iron lantern holding a live ember (the Pilgrim's Spark) hangs from his right hip. He is left-handed, and his sword **Vowbrand** is a long, narrow, single-edged dark-iron blade with a hooked back-spur near the tip, a faint glowing ember vein along the fuller, an open octagonal brass ring-guard and a leather-wrapped grip.

> *Correction (Episode 3):* left and right are given from Cael's own point of view and now match the rendered model. Earlier versions of these notes had them mirrored.

- **Silhouette signatures:** prow helm with keel fin and one-sided spur, asymmetric shoulders (layered plates on his left, half-cloak on his right), and a lantern glow at the right hip.
- **Palette:** charcoal iron `#2a2826`, ash-grey highlights, oxblood `#5a1414`, dark crimson `#781a18`, faded brass `#8a6a35`, ember orange `#ff7a22`.
- **Personality:** quiet, dutiful, haunted. A soldier who outlived his kingdom, he follows the Heart's call out of a need to matter.
- **Transformed state (the Final Keeper):** same armor, now split by molten orange-gold cracks; the V-slit blazes white-gold; flames rise from his shoulders; he sits fused to the Keeper's Seat.

### The Ashwights (recurring enemies)
> **[ASHWIGHT]** Gaunt former soldiers of Vhaldren whose inner spark went out. Elongated limbs, a hunched spine, cracked ash-grey skin with dark fissures. Rusted wide-brimmed kettle hats, the remains of rusted breastplates, ragged grey-brown tabards. Two pinpoint cold-white eyes. Broken halberds. They shed ash as they move.

### The Knellwyrm (boss), secretly the previous keeper
> **[KNELLWYRM]** A colossal serpentine beast about 80 m long, built from rounded ash-stone plates with sparse glowing ember fissures. A long eyeless skull with hollow sockets, heavy bone brow ridges and two long horns sweeping back, a hinged lower jaw of bone teeth with a dim furnace glow inside. Bone hooks rise from its spine, each hung with a cracked bronze funeral bell; the bells toll as it moves. Four long bony legs end in three-claw feet. A **rusted keel-prow helm with the brass diamond sigil** is sunk into its brow: the remains of the knight it once was.

### The Ember Heart
> **[HEART]** A vast celestial fire hanging in the sky: a faceted molten sphere half-crusted by drifting dark ash plates, haloed in dim crimson, caught in a net of four colossal iron chains that descend to the needle spire of the Ashen Cathedral. Reborn, the crust melts and it burns gold-white.

### Locations
- **Vhaldren:** a dying kingdom of ash plains, broken towers and lone arches under a crimson-charcoal sky.
- **The bridge of Odran:** an enormous stone bridge over a fog-filled abyss, with tall piers, pointed arches below and hooded statues of the **Ember Wardens** (a forgotten order) holding offering bowls aloft along both parapets.
- **The ash forest:** dead grey trees and a floor of drifting ash in dense fog.
- **The abandoned village:** half-roofed timber houses whose people stand frozen as ash statues.
- **The Ashen Cathedral:** an original gothic design with twin front towers and octagonal spires, a glowing rose window and lancet windows, flying buttresses with pinnacles, and a central needle spire about 260 m high where the chains converge.
- **The Keeper's Seat:** a massive dark-stone throne on a circular summit platform above a sea of cloud, with four brass anchor rings, one per chain. Beside it, a carved wall tells the keeper's fate in three panels.

### Sigils
- **The Weeping Ember:** a diamond outline (the Heart), an ember dot (the spark), three falling tear-lines (the ash of those who kept it). Worn on Cael's tabard.
- **The Brow Diamond:** a small brass diamond riveted to a keeper's helm. Cael has it; so does the Knellwyrm.

## I–J. How the video was actually produced

No AI image or video generation model was reachable from this production environment. Instead of faking it with prompts alone, the film was **built and rendered for real as a low-poly 3D production**. For a PS1/PS2 cinematic look, that is the most faithful method anyway:

1. **Models:** every character, creature and building is built from code as segmented low-poly meshes (`render/src/characters.js`, `render/src/world.js`), in the way PS1-era games built rigid-limb characters.
2. **Textures:** all are procedural pixel textures generated at runtime (`render/src/textures.js`): stone, ash, iron, cloth, sigils, the mural, clouds and the sun's crust.
3. **Retro renderer:** custom three.js shaders (`render/src/engine.js`) provide affine (warping) texture mapping, vertex snapping (jitter), per-vertex Gouraud lighting, distance and height fog, rim and fill lights, a 4×4 Bayer dither, 15-bit colour quantization, mip-based bloom and vignette, at an internal 360×640 resolution.
4. **Animation:** keyframed rigid-limb poses, a procedural walk cycle, cloth simulation for the cloak, a spline-driven serpent spine for the Knellwyrm, deterministic GPU particles (ash, embers, fire, bursts, line-fire along chains), a slow-motion time remap and camera shake.
5. **Rendering:** headless Chromium (SwiftShader WebGL2) renders 1,920 frames deterministically (`render/render.cjs`).
6. **Narration:** Kokoro-82M text-to-speech (Apache-2.0, open weights), then a pitch and EQ treatment in ffmpeg.
7. **Score and sound:** 100% procedural numpy synthesis (`audio/synth.py`) covering wavetable drones, formant-synthesized choir, string ostinato, taiko, inharmonic bell synthesis, roar, wind, fire, armor, chains and convolution reverb.
8. **Edit:** dissolves, flashes and fades in the renderer; burned-in Cinzel subtitles (SIL Open Font License) with ffmpeg and libass; nearest-neighbor 3× upscale to 1080×1920; low-resolution film grain; H.264 and AAC.

Rebuild everything with `./build.sh` (see `../README.md`).

Third-party components are tools under permissive licences: three.js (MIT), Kokoro-82M voice model (Apache-2.0), Cinzel font (SIL OFL 1.1) and ffmpeg. No copyrighted game assets, footage, music or voices were used.
