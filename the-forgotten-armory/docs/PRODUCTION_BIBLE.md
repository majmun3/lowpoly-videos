## H. The Vigil Plate: design notes and originality
- **The helm** (highest priority) keeps the qualities requested: fully enclosed, a rounded and slightly elongated skull, a narrow horizontal eye opening, narrow vertical ventilation slits on the lower face, strong steel construction, and weathered silver-grey with aged detail. It is built from historical armet and close-helm language. It uses **its own geometry**:
  - one continuous eye-slit with a centre bar, rather than paired slots;
  - two angled columns of four vertical breaths flanking a shallow centre keel;
  - a low rounded comb instead of swept crest plates;
  - an aged-bronze brow band carrying Cael's own brass diamond, continuity from Episodes 1–2;
  - bronze visor rivets and a three-lame gorget.
- **The body** is keeled breastplate and plackart over dark mail, three-lame faulds, symmetrical layered rounded pauldrons with bronze edges, engraved vambraces, poleyns, greaves and laminated sabatons. Charcoal cloth with a blue-grey bronze-hemmed panel, and leather belts and strap.
- **The shield's emblem**, the Watchful Ember, is geometric and original: a crown bar, a lozenge, cradling hands and three falling lines. It has no foliage, beasts or existing heraldry.
- The uploaded photographs were used only as general mood references. No existing character, armour set, helmet geometry, ornament, heraldry or shield artwork was reproduced, and no franchise names are used in any generation prompt.

## How it was produced
As in Episodes 1–2, no AI image or video model is reachable here, so everything was **rendered for real**. The armour is a new low-poly model (`render/src/vigil.js`) fitted to Cael's exact Episode 1 rig. Old and new pieces live on the same skeleton and are swapped region by region for the transformation (cloak and tabard → chest → shoulders → arms and legs → helm).

The fortress, armory and debris are in `render/src/fortress.js`. The 21 shots are in `render/src/shots.js`, and the timeline in `render/src/film.js`. Narration uses Kokoro (Apache-2.0); the score and foley are fully procedural; subtitles are Cinzel (OFL). Rebuild with `../build.sh`.
